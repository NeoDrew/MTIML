Section 8

Discussion

Tightness, scope, the dichotomy, PAC learning, modern caveats.

Tightness

The $\sqrt{\log m / m}$ rate retains a logarithmic factor that more refined techniques can remove. Chaining and Dudley's entropy integral yield bounds of the form $$\mathfrak R_m(\mathcal F) = O\!\left(\sqrt{d/m}\right)$$ without the log factor. The bound is also distribution-free; data-dependent variants based on the empirical Rademacher complexity are typically tighter in practice.

Scope

The reduction relies on $\|a\|_2 = \sqrt m$, which exploits the binary structure $\mathcal F \subseteq \{\pm 1\}^{\mathcal X}$. Real-valued classes require alternative complexity measures: the pseudo-dimension and the fat-shattering dimension generalise the framework to such settings.

The dichotomy

Sauer's Lemma reveals that growth functions exhibit only two regimes: exponential when $\mathrm{VCdim}(\mathcal F) = \infty$, and eventually polynomial when $\mathrm{VCdim}(\mathcal F) < \infty$. No intermediate growth rate is possible — a structurally striking feature of binary hypothesis classes.

Connection to PAC learning

Fundamental theorem of statistical learning

A class is PAC-learnable if and only if it has finite VC dimension, with sample complexity $$O\!\left(\frac{d}{\varepsilon^2}\,\log\frac{1}{\delta}\right).$$

VC theory thus characterises learnability itself, not merely a sufficient condition. The "if" direction is what we proved (finite VC ⇒ uniform convergence ⇒ PAC learnable). The "only if" direction is the harder half — the no-free-lunch argument shows that infinite VC dim implies no algorithm can succeed in the worst case.

Modern caveat

Deep neural networks possess effectively unbounded VC dimension yet generalise well in practice. Classical VC theory does not predict this behaviour, motivating:

  • Margin-based bounds — replace VC dim with a continuous margin parameter that captures the geometry of the data.
  • PAC-Bayes analysis (Dziugaite & Roy, 2017) — combine the prior knowledge with posterior concentration.
  • Implicit regularisation and the neural tangent kernel — reason about what SGD actually finds, not what could be found.
  • Algorithmic stability — bound generalisation through algorithm sensitivity rather than class capacity.

None of this diminishes the foundational character of the VC bound. It is the canonical distribution-free, algorithm-agnostic guarantee — every modern theory is benchmarked against it.
